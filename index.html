How it works

  • A seller locks tokens in the contract and names exactly what they want in return.
  • Anyone can fill the whole order. The seller gets exactly their price. No pools, no slippage.
  • Fee: 0.05% from the seller and 0.05% from the buyer, each taken from what they receive. No other fees.
  • Sellers can cancel anytime, free, with a full refund.
  • The contract has no owner and no admin. Nobody, including the creator, can change rules, pause it, or touch funds.
  • Only the listed tokens can be traded. Tokens with transfer taxes are rejected automatically.

p and e tokens are different

On PulseChain, tokens copied from Ethereum at the fork (pDAI, pHEX, pWBTC) are not the same as tokens bridged from Ethereum (eDAI, eHEX, eWBTC). They have very different prices. Always check the prefix and the contract address.

Where prices come from

Prices on this site are read directly from PulseX liquidity pools (PulseX V1 and V2 factories) and converted to USD through the WPLS/eDAI and WPLS/eUSDC pools. They are indicative only. Pools with low liquidity can be moved easily and may show wrong prices; these are marked thin. The contract does not use these prices. Trades happen only at the exact amounts written in each order.

Your responsibility

This is an interface to an immutable smart contract. Before creating or filling an order, check the token addresses, amounts and price yourself. Displayed prices may be delayed or wrong. Transactions on the blockchain are final and cannot be reversed. Use at your own risk. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
